Frequently Asked Questions

What age is best for spot the difference puzzles?

These puzzles work for ages 4-12, with simpler versions for younger kids and complex ones for older children.

How often should my child do visual perception activities?

Short daily sessions of 10-15 minutes are ideal for building skills without burnout.

Can these puzzles help with reading readiness?

Yes, because they train the brain to notice small details, a skill essential for letter and word recognition.

Are photorealistic puzzles better than cartoon ones?

Photorealistic scenes offer more realistic details, which help children transfer observation skills to real life.

What if my child gets frustrated?

Start with easier puzzles, offer hints, and emphasize fun. Praise effort and model how to scan systematically.
