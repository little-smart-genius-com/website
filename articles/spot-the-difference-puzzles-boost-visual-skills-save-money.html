DIY puzzles using free images

Seriously, your phone is gold. Take two photos of your living room—just move one item between shots. A coffee cup. A single shoe. Whatever.

Then sit with your child and ask: "What's different?" My 7-year-old thought this was magic the first time. We used two photos of our cat—one with a toy mouse in her mouth, one without—and had a blast finding the change. Took maybe five minutes to set up.

Free stock photo sites work too. Grab two similar images, edit one slightly in a basic photo app, then print. Voilà. Your child looks absolutely genius week.
