Frequently Asked Questions

How many differences are in each puzzle?

Each puzzle typically has 5 to 10 differences, carefully chosen to be challenging but achievable for children aged 4-12.

Can I use these puzzles for a classroom or group activity?

Absolutely! They work well for partner work, small groups, or as a calm-down activity. The photorealistic animals engage a wide age range.

Do I need to print these or can I use them on a tablet?

You can print them for a screen-free option or use them on a tablet with a drawing app. Either way works great for bonding time.

What if my child finds all the differences too quickly?

Encourage them to describe each difference in detail, or time themselves to beat their own record. You can also create your own differences on the page for extra fun.

Are these puzzles based on real animals?

Yes! The images are photorealistic, making them more engaging and educational than cartoon versions. Kids learn to recognize real animals and their features.
