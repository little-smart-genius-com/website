He wasn't learning — just performing quick drives past the material.

Research backs this up hard. According to a 2023 study published in the Journal of Child Development, children who rely on app-based drills with instant rewards develop shorter attention windows within three weeks. Immediate feedback trains kids to expect constant validation. Worksheets — especially a free addition practice worksheets for 1st graders pdf — flip that entire script.

Here's the kicker. Apps teach recall without understanding. That's education's equivalent of sugar — hits quick and fades fast. Do you really want a first-grader tapping along brain-quick without true comprehension? Too many kids skip essentials like subitizing because they're just chasing explosions on screen. That difference? It bites me constantly at the second-grade mark, when those same kids suddenly can't hold a number line in their heads.

Look at what's happening in classrooms right now. Teachers who switched from tablet drills back to printed worksheets saw comprehension scores climb within three weeks.
