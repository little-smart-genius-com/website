The key is starting simple. Print just one puzzle, grab a pencil (bonus points for one with a cute topper—less crying, I promise), and sit side by side. You don’t need to explain everything at once. Let your child guess, erase, and try again. The act of crossing out wrong answers builds mental math in a way flash cards never could.
